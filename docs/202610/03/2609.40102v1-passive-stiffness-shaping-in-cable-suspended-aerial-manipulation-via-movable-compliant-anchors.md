## 摘要
缆索悬吊式空中操作为协同运输和物理交互提供了一种轻量化架构，但负载处所感知的被动机械响应仍未被充分理解并得到系统性利用。本工作将飞行器解释为可移动柔顺锚点，并建立了一种重力感知的准静态理论，用于预测和塑造悬吊负载的被动笛卡尔刚度。该公式适用于任意数量的飞行器通过绷紧、笔直、不可伸长的缆索连接到点负载的情形。在选定的重力加载平衡状态下，空中锚点柔顺性与缆索横向几何柔顺性在每条支腿内串联组合，而各支腿刚度则并联作用于负载。对于各向同性的空中锚点行为，每条支腿都精确等效于一根虚拟的单向弹性缆索，从而揭示出由平衡张力支配的轴向—横向刚度分解。这些结果定义了一个从指令锚点构型到被动负载刚度的非线性映射，其微分使得能够通过锚点重定位进行局部保约束整形。本文定义了一个动态刚体验证框架，该框架具有非线性飞行器控制、弹性阻尼腱以及环境接触，用于评估所推导刚度在超出解析模型假设时何时以及在多大程度上仍具有预测性。

## Abstract
Cable-suspended aerial manipulation offers a lightweight architecture for cooperative transportation and physical interaction, yet the passive mechanical response perceived at the load remains insufficiently understood and systematically exploited. This work interprets aerial vehicles as movable compliant anchors and develops a gravity-aware quasi-static theory for predicting and shaping the passive Cartesian stiffness of a suspended load. The formulation applies to an arbitrary number of aerial vehicles connected to a point load by taut, straight, inextensible cables. At a selected gravity-loaded equilibrium, aerial-anchor compliance and transverse cable geometric compliance combine in series within each leg, while the leg stiffnesses act in parallel on the load. For isotropic aerial-anchor behavior, each leg is exactly equivalent to a virtual unilateral elastic cable, revealing an axial--transverse stiffness decomposition governed by the equilibrium tension. These results define a nonlinear map from commanded-anchor configuration to passive load stiffness, whose differential enables local constraint-preserving shaping through anchor repositioning. A dynamic rigid-body validation framework with nonlinear vehicle control, elastic-damped tendons, and environmental contact is defined to assess when and to what extent the derived stiffness remains predictive beyond the assumptions of the analytical model.

## 2. 方法论：核心思想、技术细节与公式流程

- **核心视角**：把每架无人机视为**可移动的柔顺锚点（movable compliant anchor）**，其指令位置决定悬吊负载处感知的被动笛卡尔刚度。
- **系统模型**：

- **单支腿的串联柔顺（关键结构）**：

  - 锚点柔顺与几何柔顺**串联**：−δp = (Cp,ᵢ + Cgeo,ᵢ)(δφᵢ)。
  - **定理 1（单腿被动刚度）**：Kᵢ := (Cp,ᵢ + Cgeo,ᵢ)⁻¹，对称正定，恢复力关系 δφᵢ = −Kᵢ(δp)。

- **各向同性锚点的精确等价（重要理论结果）**：
