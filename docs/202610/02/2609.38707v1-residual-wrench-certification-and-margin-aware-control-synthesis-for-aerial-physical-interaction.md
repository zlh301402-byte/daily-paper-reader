## 摘要
我们提出一个任务相关框架，用于认证具有有界执行器的多旋翼在悬停与接触载荷后的残余力旋量能力。利用凸几何，我们为指定的凸储备（包括欧氏球和加权椭球）推导带符号裕度。我们从执行器内部性界获得可计算的储备证书，以支持松弛最大化分配。为保持所需储备，我们提出将指令投影到收紧的可行集上。随后，我们将可用储备与结构化增益综合联系起来，并认证一个满足执行器限制的局部跟踪区域。在该区域内，我们建立标称指数收敛和鲁棒最终有界性。对于指定任务与形态族，我们表明在相同总推力下，优化后的八旋翼比优化后的六旋翼获得更大的裕度。我们使用固定几何八旋翼和安装推力匹配的可变倾转四旋翼，在持续刚性壁接触的闭环仿真中评估所提框架。对于指定任务族，四旋翼的局部证书允许更高的归一化推压极限。在超出可实现性边界的测试中，我们观察到预测的能力丧失：八旋翼受旋翼推力限制，四旋翼受倾转伺服限制。

## Abstract
We develop a task-relative framework for certifying residual wrench authority after hover and contact loading in multirotors with bounded actuators. Using convex geometry, we derive signed margins for prescribed convex reserves, including Euclidean balls and weighted ellipsoids. We obtain computable reserve certificates from actuator-interiority bounds to support slack-maximizing allocation. To preserve the required reserve, we propose command projection onto a tightened feasible set. We then connect the available reserve to structured gain synthesis and certify a local tracking region that respects actuator limits. Within this region, we establish nominal exponential convergence and robust ultimate boundedness. For the prescribed task and morphology family, we show that the optimized octarotor attains a larger margin than the optimized hexarotor at equal total thrust. We evaluate the proposed framework in closed-loop simulations of sustained rigid-wall contact using a fixed-geometry octarotor and a variable-tilt quadrotor with matched installed thrust. The quadrotor's local certificate admits a higher normalized push limit for the prescribed task family. In tests beyond the realizability boundaries, we observe the predicted loss of authority through rotor-thrust limits for the octarotor and tilt-servo limits for the quadrotor.
